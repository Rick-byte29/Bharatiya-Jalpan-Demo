The Bharatiya Jalpan story

1985 was not a launch.
It was the first page.

Some places sell sweets. Others become part of how a town celebrates. Bharatiya Jalpan belongs to the second kind.

Across birthdays, pujas, weddings, homecomings, office celebrations and “just one box for the road” moments, the ritual stays familiar: choose your favourites, watch the box fill, carry the fragrance home.

Our identity is proudly Indian and unmistakably local — a Bongaigaon name built on consistency, warmth and the timeless pleasure of mithai shared with people who matter.

The mithai gallery

Twenty-four classics.
One very difficult choice.

Rich, syrupy, nutty, milky, flaky, soft — Indian sweets have a texture for every mood. Here is a celebratory selection of favourites to inspire your next box.

Cashew classic01

Kaju Katli

Silky cashew diamonds with the clean richness that makes one piece become three.

Festive icon02

Motichoor Ladoo

Fine golden pearls gathered into the quintessential celebration sweet.

Soft & syrupy03

Rasgulla

Cloud-soft chhena spheres resting in a bright, delicate syrup.

Warm favourite04

Gulab Jamun

Deep caramel notes, tender khoya and fragrant syrup in every spoonful.

Fresh favourite05

Jalebi

Crisp spirals, bright saffron colour and syrup that crackles before it melts.

Royal creaminess06

Rasmalai

Soft chhena in aromatic milk, finished with saffron and nuts.

Bengal favourite07

Sandesh

Delicate chhena sweetness with a clean, melt-away finish.

Milk-rich08

Kalakand

Soft, grainy milk fudge with a slow-cooked richness.

Caramel milk09

Milk Cake

Dense, milky and beautifully caramelised through the centre.

Saffron-kissed10

Kesar Peda

Velvety milk sweetness lifted with saffron and cardamom.

Flaky classic11

Soan Papdi

Feathery layers that crumble, dissolve and disappear almost instantly.

Ghee-roasted12

Besan Ladoo

Roasted gram flour, ghee and cardamom rolled into pure nostalgia.

Nutty indulgence13

Kaju Roll

Cashew-rich rolls with festive polish and a luxurious bite.

Almond rich14

Badam Barfi

Dense almond sweetness with a refined, nut-forward finish.

Soft & creamy15

Malai Cham Cham

Supple chhena, soft sweetness and a creamy festive finish.

Celebration bowl16

Rajbhog

A generous, saffron-toned chhena sweet made for grand occasions.

Crunch & syrup17

Imarti

Intricate rings with a crisp shell and syrup-soaked centre.

Flaky & glazed18

Balushahi

Crumbly, layered richness finished in a translucent sugar glaze.

Festival pastry19

Gujiya

Golden pastry pockets with a rich, celebratory filling.

Festive showstopper20

Ghewar

Honeycomb crispness, creamy toppings and a plate made for the centre of the table.

Pan-fried classic21

Malpua

Soft-centred, caramel-edged and finished with fragrant syrup.

Ghee-rich22

Mysore Pak

Bold ghee richness with a texture that ranges from fudgy to delicately crumbly.

Festive favourite23

Modak

A beloved celebratory sweet with a distinctive crown-shaped form.

Winter comfort24

Gajar Halwa

Slow-cooked carrot, milk, ghee and nuts — warm, rich and deeply familiar.
